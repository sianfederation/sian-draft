# DRAFT-Johan-0: Atoms and Voluntary Spread

Modern social networks tend to spread and inflame hate and abuse. They fracture our society and boost racism, nationalism and other forms of insularity. Rage increases retention, so this policy helps them serve more ads, direct the users to make more purchases and otherwise make more profit.

The anonymity of an avatar can shield individual users from retribution from authorities, bosses and other hierarchical institutions of violence. On the flip-side bad actors such as the spreaders of misinformation and hate, intimidators, and abusers can be protected as well. Hopefully, grid-node moderators will choose policies that slow down the spread of the hate and impede bullying.

The structure of the system should allow each grid-node to ignore, ban or block any content or user that they find abusive, offensive or simply cannot allow themselves to platform. Content should spread from node to node in the form of *atoms* — indivisible packets of information that are posted on bulletin boards with an expiration date. A node that receives an atom can choose whether to post it on its board and send it on to its neighbors, or to discard it and avoid being associated with it. If enough nodes block a hateful user, they will not be able to spread their abuse at all.

This also makes flood-advertising on Siân less actionable.
